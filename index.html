Research focus

I examine how AI systems can be updated, deployed, and evaluated under real-world security, privacy, and governance constraints.

Research perspective

AI systems should be able to change without becoming harder to trust.

My work brings together machine unlearning, privacy-aware inference, agentic security, and auditable model updates. I develop methods and evaluations that make system changes easier to inspect, explain, and govern.
